How LITFREIGHT Works

Whether you are sourcing products or offering them, get started with a straightforward process.

01

Discover or request

Browse product listings or send a buying requirement using our short guest request form.

02

Admin review

Public business listings and guest buying requests are checked by the admin before publishing.

03

Connect & negotiate

Buyers and suppliers can exchange enquiries, discuss quantities and request quotations.

04

Arrange freight

Request a freight quote and coordinate suitable shipping services for your trade.
